Tabs that sit before your chat rooms. They need no server and no sign-in, and nothing in them reaches a chat room unless you share it.

| Room | Shows | Tab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [◔ Usage](#-usage) | Context, rate limits, spend, tool timings | on |
| [⎇ Git](#-git) | Your branch, its PR and checks, Actions | on |
| [⟡ Agents](#-agents) | Every Claude Code session and its subagents | on |
| [◷ Focus](#-focus) | A Pomodoro timer with a streak | on |
| [✓ Habits](#-habits) | Daily habits, each with its streak | on |
| [⚒ Toolbox](#-toolbox) | Base64, URL, JWT, UUID, hashes, Unix times, cron, JSON and more | on |
| [✿ Pet](#-pet) | Clawd, moving along with your session | on |
| [⌘ Snippets](#-snippets) | Code you reuse, one click from the clipboard | on |
| [▦ Monitor](#-monitor) | CPU, memory, network, battery | on |
| [⧉ Clipboard](#-clipboard) | What you copied lately | `/chat rooms +clipboard` |
| [☀ Weather](#-weather) | Now, 24 hours and the week | `/chat rooms +weather` |
| [✦ Tech News](#-tech-news) | Hacker News and tech feeds | `/chat rooms +news` |
| [$ Stocks](#-stocks) | Taiwan and US quotes | `/chat rooms +stock` |
| [♫ Lo-fi](#-lo-fi) | Music while you work | `/chat rooms +lofi` |

Open one with its tab, or `/chat <room>` (`/chat usage`, `/chat stock`). The last five reach the web or your clipboard, so they wait until you add them. On the desktop app, **+** in the header adds one too.

## ◔ Usage

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/usage.png" alt="The Usage room: context and the 5-hour and 7-day meters, spend with its chart and cache hits, each tool's p50 and p95, and an Explore subagent." /></figure>

How this session uses Claude, live.

- **Context, 5-hour, 7-day**: how full each is, and when it resets.
- **Spend**: cost, the rate per hour, tokens and the cache.
- **Tools and subagents**: each tool's p50, p95 and failures, and what each subagent does.

## ⎇ Git

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/git.png" alt="The Git room: the branch and its changes, this branch's pull request, other open pull requests, the latest Actions runs, and issues and alerts." /></figure>

Where your branch stands on GitHub, through the [`gh` CLI](https://cli.github.com).

- **Branch**: ahead and behind, files changed, and a commit graph on the desktop.
- **This PR**: checks as they run, reviews, whether it can merge.
- **Toasts** when checks fail or pass, or a review comes in. `r` refreshes.

## ⟡ Agents

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/agents.png" alt="The Agents room: sessions on this computer with their subagents as a tree, and the live feed of tool calls." /></figure>

Every Claude Code session on this computer.

- **Sessions**: folder, branch, model, and what each is doing now.
- **Subagents** as a tree under the session that started them.
- **Live feed** of tool calls from all of them.

## ◷ Focus

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/focus.png" alt="The Focus room: a 50-minute round running with Pause, Break and reset, the lengths, and the streak." /></figure>

A Pomodoro timer.

- **▶ Start**, **⏸**, **⏭** and **↺**, with a chime at each end. Type `50 10` (or `/focus 50 10`) for your lengths.
- **A streak** of the days you finished a round, drawn like GitHub's.
- `🍅 18:42` in the status line while it runs.

## ✓ Habits

Habits you keep every day, up to 12.

- **Add one** by typing it in the room's box and pressing **add**: `Read 30 min`, or `Water x8` for one you do 8 times a day. `remove Run`, `rename Read -> Read 20 min` and `undo Water` change them, and `/habit` does it all from the prompt.
- **✓** checks a habit off for today (again takes it back), and **+1** and **−** count one you do more than once. **✕** on a card deletes the habit and its streak.
- **A streak** for each habit, drawn like GitHub's: the months, Mon, Wed and Fri, today ringed, and a shade for how many times on a counted one.
- **The band** shows how many are done today, the longest streak running and what's left. The tab counts what's left from other rooms.

## ⚒ Toolbox

Sixteen small tools a developer reaches for, after [DevToys](https://devtoys.app/), in four groups:

| Group | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Encode | Base64, URL, HTML, JWT (read, the signature not checked) |
| Generate | UUID (v4 or v7), ULID, Hash (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512), Password, Lorem |
| Convert | Time (Unix seconds or ms, dates), Base (dec, hex, oct, bin), Cron (read aloud, the next runs), JSON |
| Text | Escape, Count, Case |

- **Pick a tool** from the menu, then type what it runs on in the room's box. A generator takes its options there: `5 v7` for five UUID v7s, `24 nosym` for a password. A tool's name alone switches to it.
- **⧉** copies a result, **⧉ all** every UUID at once, and **⧉ paste** reads the clipboard into the tool. A password stays masked until **show**.
- **`/tool`** does it from the prompt: `/tool hash hello`, `/tool time 1739000000`, `/tool cron */15 9-18 * * 1-5`.
- **The band** shows the last result, cut short (a password masked). With the Clipboard room on, it shows a JWT you just copied with its time left, or a Unix time as a date, and the tab shows **!** when that JWT has under 10 minutes.
- **Kept nowhere**: what you type and what comes out live in the session's memory, and the room has no Share.

## ✿ Pet

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/pet.png" alt="The Pet room: Clawd with headphones, time together, the focus rounds today, the mood, the log and the mood picker." /></figure>

Clawd's corner. Its mood follows the session: typing while Claude works, cheering a finished round, asleep after a quiet while.

- **Today together** and a **bond** that levels up.
- **Lately**: what Clawd noticed (never what anyone wrote).
- **By the input box**: Clawd strolls, stops and goes, sits, or stays off.

## ⌘ Snippets

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/snippet.png" alt="The Snippets room: three saved snippets, each with copy and share." /></figure>

Code you reach for again and again.

- `/snippet add` saves your selection, or Claude's last code block. `/snippet add <text>` saves text.
- **⧉** copies, **⇪** shares to a chat room.
- Kept in `~/.config/squad-chat/room-data/snippet/list.json`, readable only by you.

## ▦ Monitor

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/monitor.png" alt="The Monitor room: CPU with load and temperature, memory and swap, GPU and power with the fans, and the network chart." /></figure>

This computer as it runs, with no sudo: CPU, memory, GPU, network, disk and battery. Temperatures and power need [macmon](https://github.com/vladkens/macmon) on a Mac (`brew install macmon`). It toasts when something runs hot or nearly full. `/chat set monitor alerts off` stops that.

## ⧉ Clipboard

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/clipboard.png" alt="The Clipboard room: four copied items, newest first, a Supabase key among them shown masked." /></figure>

The last 30 things you copied, to copy again. Kept in memory only, gone when the session ends. Anything that looks like a key shows masked, and nothing here can be shared.

## ☀ Weather

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/weather.png" alt="The Weather room: Taipei now, the next 24 hours, this week and sunrise and sunset." /></figure>

Your cities from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), no key: now, the next 24 hours, the week, sunrise and sunset. Type a city in the room's box to add it, ✕ drops one. `/chat set weather units imperial` switches units.

## ✦ Tech News

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/news.png" alt="The Tech News room: Hacker News stories with their scores and comments, then the feeds." /></figure>

[Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) and tech feeds, newest first. **⧉** copies a link, **⇪** shares it. `/chat set news hn best` picks the list.

## $ Stocks

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/stock.png" alt="The Stocks room: TAIEX, 2330, 0050, the S&P 500, Apple and NVIDIA, each with its change and the day's line." /></figure>

A watchlist of Taiwan and US stocks, each with today's line. Set it with `/chat set stock watchlist TAIEX, 2330, AAPL` (up to 12). Prices may be delayed, and none of it is investment advice.

## ♫ Lo-fi

<figure class="shot pane"><img src="/squad-chat/media/rooms/lofi.png" alt="The Lo-fi room: the player and its volume, and the stations." /></figure>

Lofi Girl and SomaFM through [mpv](https://mpv.io) and [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) (`brew install mpv yt-dlp`). Paste a link or a folder into the box to add your own. `p` plays or pauses, `n` skips. Nothing plays until you press play.

## More rooms, sharing, hiding

- **[The room store](/squad-chat/rooms/store/)**: `/chat store` lists rooms anyone wrote, `/chat install <id>` adds one. A room is one JSON file, so installing one never runs code.
- **Share a snapshot**: **⇪ Share** under a room, or `/chat-share usage #room`, posts it as a card after a preview.
- **Pick your tabs**: `/chat rooms usage,git`, `+clipboard`, `-snippet` or `none`.

Every setting is in [Commands](/squad-chat/use/commands/). What each room reads is in [Privacy & security](/squad-chat/reference/privacy/#what-the-built-in-rooms-read).
